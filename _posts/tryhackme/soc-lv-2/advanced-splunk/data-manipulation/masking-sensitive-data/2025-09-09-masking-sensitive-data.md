Masking sensitive fields, such as credit card numbers, is crucial for maintaining compliance with standards like the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) and the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Splunk offers features such as field masking and anonymization to safeguard sensitive data.
