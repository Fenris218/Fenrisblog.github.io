## Alert Triage

 The triage is focused on analyzing the specific alert. This determines the severity of the alert and helps us prioritize it. The alert triage is all about answering the 5 Ws. (What, When, Where, Who, Why)

## Reporting

The detected harmful alerts need to be escalated to higher-level analysts for a timely response and resolution. These alerts are escalated as tickets and assigned to the relevant people.

## **Incident Response and Forensics**

This forensic activity aims to determine the incident’s root cause by analyzing the artifacts from a system or network.
