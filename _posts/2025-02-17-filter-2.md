- Display filter Expressions : giúp filter khi không nhớ cú pháp **Analyse --> Display Filter Expression**

![image.png](image%201.png)

- **Filter: "contains": http.server contains "Apache”**
- **Filter: "matches" để dùng regex : http.host matches "\.(php|html)”**
- **Filter: "in" : tcp.port in {80 443 8080}**
- **Filter: "upper/lower" :** `upper(http.server) contains "APACHE"`
- **Filter: "string" : string(frame.number) matches "[13579]$”**
